# Rheel Technology Limited — Privacy Notice

**Internal draft — publication still depends on the retention deletion workflow and operational checks described below.**  
Drafted: 25 October 2026.  
The operational retention periods below were approved for drafting by the company on 25 October 2026. They are internal policy choices, not statutory periods.

Rheel Technology Limited is the organisation responsible for personal data collected through the RheelTech website and careers portal.

## 2. Information we collect and why

### Contact enquiries

If you contact us, we process your name, email address, organisation (if supplied), enquiry type, and message so we can respond and follow up. These details are sent to a designated RheelTech inbox through the website’s submission service. The inquiry is not stored in the careers database.

We process enquiries on the basis of our legitimate interests in responding to and managing business enquiries. Where an enquiry relates to a potential contract, we may also process information to take steps requested by you before entering into that contract.

### Job applications

If you apply for a vacancy, we process your name, email address, phone number, location, experience summary, cover letter, CV, the vacancy you applied for, and the date you acknowledged the privacy notice. We use these details to receive, assess, and manage your application and communicate about the recruitment process.

Application details are stored in a Supabase database. CVs are stored in a private file bucket, restricted to authorised recruitment staff and administrators. Recruitment notification emails contain the candidate’s name and email address; CVs and cover letters are not attached.

We process application information to take steps requested by you in connection with an application, on the basis of our legitimate interests in carrying out recruitment, and where required to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

### Staff accounts and access records

For authorised staff accounts, we process work email, display name, role, account status, and staff-management audit events. We use these details to control staff access, administer the portal, maintain security, and keep an access-change history.

We process staff account and audit information on the basis of our legitimate interests in operating and securing the staff portal and, where applicable, to meet legal obligations.

## 3. Service providers and international processing

We use service providers to operate the website and its workflows. The current technical setup includes:

- **GitHub Pages** to serve the public website.
- **Cloudflare** for the public careers submission gateway.
- **Supabase** for the database, authentication, private CV storage, and server-side functions. The production project is hosted in the EU Ireland region.
- **Resend** to send contact and recruitment emails. Resend states that email data is stored in the United States.
- **Google Fonts** is currently loaded from Google’s servers to display website typography.

Zoho Mail hosts the designated RheelTech inboxes that receive contact and recruitment notifications. The company has confirmed that inbox copies follow the 12-month enquiry retention rule, including forwarding and archive copies.

Some service providers may process information outside Nigeria, including in the EU, United States, or other countries. RheelTech has confirmed that the applicable transfer grounds and safeguards have been reviewed and documented with its adviser. The current project’s selected Supabase region does not by itself establish that every provider operation or log stays in that region.

## 4. How long we keep information

Subject to legal holds and any applicable legal obligations, our approved operational schedule is:

- **Unsuccessful or withdrawn applications and CVs:** 12 months after the final recruitment decision for the vacancy, then delete the application record and CV.
- **Contact enquiries:** 12 months after the last substantive response or resolution, including copies in the designated mailbox and its forwarding/archive locations.
- **Staff access audit entries:** 24 months after the event, then delete or irreversibly de-identify.
- **Active staff account and profile information:** while access is required. Review promptly when a staff member leaves or access is no longer needed; retain only information with a documented security or legal purpose.
- **Successful candidates:** move required records into a separately governed employee-record process; this applicant schedule does not determine employee-record retention.
- **Backups and provider logs:** provider backup and log copies may persist until their documented expiry after deletion from the live system. We will restrict access and record each provider’s applicable retention window.

If a legal claim, investigation, or other lawful hold applies, we restrict the affected information, keep only what is necessary, document the reason, and review the hold periodically.

## 5. Your data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions in applicable law, you may ask us to:

- confirm whether we process your personal data and provide access to it;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
- erase information or restrict processing;
- object to processing or request data portability;
- withdraw consent where consent is the basis for that processing; and
- provide information about relevant automated decision-making.

We currently use the careers portal to support human recruitment review; it is not designed to make recruitment decisions solely through automated processing.

To make a request, email [hello@rheeltech.ng](mailto:hello@rheeltech.ng) with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that you are the person concerned. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any lawful reason we cannot fulfil a request.

You may also complain to the [Nigeria Data Protection Commission](https://ndpc.gov.ng/) if you are concerned about how your personal data is handled.

## 6. Security

We use access controls, private file storage, server-side validation, and restricted staff access to protect personal data. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. If a personal-data incident occurs, we will assess and respond in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Cookies, storage, and website technologies

The careers application flow uses browser session storage to retain a submission retry key. Supabase authentication may store session information for staff sign-in. The current site also requests font files from Google Fonts. The company has confirmed that no analytics or non-essential tracking are enabled.
