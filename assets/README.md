The supplied production assets are copied into `public/assets/` and used directly by the website:

- `rheeltech-logo.png` — header and footer wordmark.
- `rheeltech-icon.png` — connected-products hero mark and favicon.
- `rheel-app-icon.png` — Rheel App identity in the product card and hero network.
- `fyndr-logo.png`, `fyndr-icon.png` — Fyndr wordmark and network icon.
- `awaown-logo.png`, `awaown-icon.jpeg` — AwaOwn wordmark and network icon.
- `rheel-app-screen.png`, `fyndr-app-screen.png`, `awaown-app-screen.png` — product detail phone screens.

Keep originals intact. If replacing a file, retain the same filename and provide a transparent PNG where the logo artwork requires a transparent background. Images are displayed with `object-fit: contain` or cropped only through transparent padding, preserving their proportions.

Still recommended if available: an approved 1200×630 `og-rheeltech.webp` share image and an approved founder portrait (only with consent and verified attribution).
